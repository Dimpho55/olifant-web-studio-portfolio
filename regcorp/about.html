About REGCORP PTY LTD

Company Overview

REGCORP PTY LTD is a South African Emerging Multi-Discipline Company established in June 2015. The company was formed with the sole purpose of delivering world class service in the Technology sector to all its clients thereby creating jobs opportunities mostly in previously disadvantaged communities and thus contributing to the South African Economy.

The diversity of the company and its directors enables us to tackle various Commodity groups as we aim to play a part in eradicating unemployment to the Youth in our society or Community.

Our Commitment

Our Company prides itself in only employing highly skilled personnel with the right business acumen in order to enable us to complete all projects timeously and thereby transferring the necessary skills to the community members employed on those projects.
