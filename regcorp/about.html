About REGCORP

Building Excellence Since 2015

Company Overview

REGCORP PTY LTD is a South African Emerging Multi-Discipline Company established in June 2015. The company was formed with the sole purpose of delivering world-class service in the Technology sector to all its clients thereby creating job opportunities mostly in previously disadvantaged communities.

Our Foundation

The diversity of the company and its directors enables us to tackle various commodity groups as we aim to play a part in eradicating unemployment to the Youth in our society. We believe in employing highly skilled personnel with the right business acumen to enable us to complete all projects timeously and thereby transfer necessary skills to the community members we employ.

Key Facts

  • Established: June 2015
  • Status: Emerging Multi-Discipline Company
  • Geographic Reach: All 9 Provinces in South Africa
  • Focus: Technology & Food & Beverages
  • Mission: World-class turnkey solutions
  • Vision: Exceed client expectations

Geographical Footprint

REGCORP has established its footprint and can execute any project or assignment in any of the nine provinces of South Africa.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a Company that is able to provide Turnkey Technology services that exceed our client expectations and create lasting value for all stakeholders.

Mission

We aim to provide world-class Technology service that by far exceed our client expectations while creating employment and contributing to the South African economy.
